Dataset Summary – Superstore Sales:

This dataset contains sales records from a retail superstore, covering order transactions across multiple regions and product categories. It includes detailed information on:

Order dates, customer demographics, and product details

Sales, profit, quantity, and discounts per transaction

Geographical distribution across four main regions: West, East, Central, and South

Product categorization into Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology

The dataset offers valuable insights into regional performance, category trends, and seasonal sales patterns, making it ideal for sales analysis and business intelligence reporting.
